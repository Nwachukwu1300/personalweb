How I work

Four steps. One thread.

The same approach whether it's a charity's stock room, a hiring process or a national road network.

01 / 04

01 · Find the knot

I start with the people doing the work, not the technology. Where does their time go? What keeps breaking?

At Projekt Rising that meant mapping how three businesses actually ran before writing a line of code.
02 · Pull one thread

I pick the change worth the most and prototype it fast, so everyone is reacting to something real.

At SalesAPE I took 3 prototypes to leadership. 2 were greenlit.
03 · Build it straight

Then I build it properly, end to end: the data, the backend, and the part people actually click.

For Samaritan's Purse that meant replacing a spreadsheet with an order form, payments and a live stock dashboard.
04 · Prove it holds

I don't call it done until the numbers say so: tests, evaluations and real runs.

The Ventureship hiring pipeline went through 3 rounds of production test runs before it was trusted.

The knotDeclined card payments get retried with the same rules for every bank, so retries are wasted and good sales are lost.
